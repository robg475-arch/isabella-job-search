### General Job Sites (Entry-Level / New Graduate)
Search these for entry-level and new graduate positions in Atlanta Metro / North Georgia:

#### Indeed
- **URL**: https://www.indeed.com/jobs?l=Atlanta%2C+GA&sc=0kf%3Aexplvl%28ENTRY_LEVEL%29%3B
- **Filters**: Entry Level, 35 mile radius
- **Search terms**: entry level, new graduate, recent college graduate, coordinator, assistant

#### LinkedIn Jobs
- **URL**: https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/search/?location=Atlanta%2C%20GA&f_E=2
- **Filters**: Entry Level (f_E=2), Past Week
- **Search terms**: entry level, new graduate, junior, coordinator

#### ZipRecruiter
- **URL**: https://www.ziprecruiter.com/jobs-search?location=Atlanta%2C+GA
- **Filters**: 35 mile radius, Past 7 days
- **Search terms**: entry level, new graduate, administrative assistant, program coordinator
