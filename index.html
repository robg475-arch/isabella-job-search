Tips for Your Job Search, Isabella 🎓
🏛️
Check Government Sites Directly
Cherokee County, Cobb County, and City of Woodstock all post positions on their own websites. Visit their HR pages regularly — government jobs often aren't on Indeed.
🔬
GBI Is a Top Employer
The Georgia Bureau of Investigation Division of Forensic Sciences regularly hires entry-level lab technicians. Bookmark their careers page and check it weekly.
📧
Set Up Job Alerts
On Indeed and LinkedIn, set an alert for "criminal justice," "forensic technician," and "victim advocate" in the Woodstock/Canton area so new jobs hit your inbox immediately.
🤝
Network Through UGA
UGA's Criminal Justice alumni network and career center are great resources. Professors also often have contacts at state agencies like GBI and local DA offices.
📄
Tailor Your Resume
Highlight coursework in forensics, research methods, and any internships or ride-alongs. Government positions look for specific criminal justice terminology in applications.
🦅
Don't Skip Federal Jobs
USAJobs.gov lists federal openings with the FBI, DEA, ATF, and U.S. Probation Office. Federal positions come with excellent benefits and career advancement paths.
